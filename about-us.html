Who We Are

Leading Multi-Speciality Hospital in Padur

Supreme Specialty Hospitals Private Limited is an integrated healthcare delivery service provider in Chennai. We currently operate our services in Padur, OMR, Chennai, and Vandalur – Kelambakkam road at Kandigai.

Supreme Hospitals, located in Padur, Kelambakkam, is a 50-bed multispeciality hospital renowned for its exceptional hospitality and world-class infrastructure. Offering a wide range of medical services, Supreme Hospitals ensures top-notch care with state-of-the-art facilities and a dedicated team of healthcare professionals.

Patients can expect personalized attention and comprehensive treatment, making it a trusted choice for healthcare in the region.
